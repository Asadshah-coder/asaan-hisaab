FAQs

What is EMI?

Equated Monthly Installment — a fixed amount you pay every month towards your loan, covering both principal and interest.

How can I reduce my EMI?

Choose a longer tenure (pay more interest overall), negotiate a lower rate, or make a bigger down payment.
