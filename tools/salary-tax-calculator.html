FAQs

What is the tax-free salary limit in Pakistan?

Annual salary up to PKR 600,000 (PKR 50,000/month) is fully tax-free for salaried individuals in FY 2026-27.

How is salary tax deducted?

Your employer deducts 1/12th of your estimated annual tax from each month's salary under Section 149 and deposits it with FBR.

What changed in Budget 2026-27?

Rates were cut in four slabs (e.g. 23%→20%, 30%→25%), new intermediate slabs were added, and the top 35% rate now applies only above PKR 7 million.
